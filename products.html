Advanced fire suppression

Clean-agent and specialized suppression systems

Designed for sensitive spaces where standard water discharge may damage equipment, documents, or operations.

  • Water Mist Systems: Eco-friendly suppression using micro-droplets with minimal water usage and fast heat absorption.
  • Pre-Action Sprinkler Systems: A controlled sprinkler solution for data centers, archives, server rooms, and other sensitive areas.
  • Novec 1230 & FM-200 Systems: Gas-based suppression for banks, lockers, electrical panels, server rooms, and critical equipment rooms.
  • Design & Commissioning: Support for cylinder placement, nozzle layout, panel integration, testing, and handover.

Passive fire protection

Fire Rated Doors

Certified fire doors help compartmentalize buildings, control smoke spread, and support safer evacuation routes.

  • Certified Fire Ratings: Door assemblies available for 60-120 minute fire and smoke resistance requirements.
  • Material Options: Galvanized Steel (GI) and wooden fire doors selected by site use, finish, and compliance need.
  • Premium Hardware: Panic bars, hydraulic closers, vision glass, hinges, tower bolts, and intumescent smoke seals.
  • Project Fitment: Suitable for staircases, lift lobbies, electrical rooms, basements, corridors, and exits.
