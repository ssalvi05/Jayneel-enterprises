Core services

Engineered systems, maintained for real readiness

Fire Hydrant & Sprinkler Systems

  • System designing and installation compliant with NBC and government regulations.
  • Annual Maintenance Contract with periodic inspections.
  • High-pressure pump room maintenance for hydrant, jockey, diesel, and panel systems.
  • Pressure testing, leakage rectification, and welding repairs.
  • Certified replacement of hydrant valves, sprinkler heads, hose pipes, and hose boxes.

Fire Alarm & Detection Systems

  • Fire-resistant cable wiring and precise detector connections.
  • Addressable panel programming and zoning for instant location identification.
  • Smoke testing, hooter decibel checks, and periodic calibration.
  • Fault rectification for open circuit, short circuit, and earth fault errors.
  • Battery backup testing and sensor cleaning to reduce false alarms.
